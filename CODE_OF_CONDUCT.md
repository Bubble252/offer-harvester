# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our Commitment

We want Offer Harvester to be a respectful, evidence-minded project for students and contributors.
Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, publication of private application data, or pressure to
share credentials is not acceptable.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss implementation, evidence, and risks in good faith.
- Keep student records, advisor contact information, and private application materials out of
  issues, pull requests, screenshots, and examples.
- State uncertainty instead of presenting unverified policy, advisor, or reputation claims as fact.
- Respect source terms, rate limits, and platform access rules.

## Reporting

For a security or privacy concern, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For conduct concerns, open a
private repository contact channel when one is configured; do not publish sensitive details in a
public issue.
