# Expected Output Shape / 预期输出结构

This fully synthetic example documents the report contract, not a factual advisor assessment.

- `candidate_status`: `candidate` or `needs_review`
- `summary`: identity, research-direction, and source-coverage summary
- `unknowns`: source gaps that require manual review
- `review_questions`: questions to resolve before outreach
- `risk_signal_policy`: community material remains a review signal, never a confirmed fact
- `truth_source_refs` and `risk_tags`: traceable source and caution labels

The report is `candidate-only` and `no-send`. It cannot contact an advisor, write an
application outcome, or promote community content into confirmed facts.

本完全虚构示例只说明报告契约，不是对真实导师的事实判断。社区内容只能作为风险信号，
结果不能联系导师、写入申请 outcome 或提升为 confirmed fact。
