# Interview Prep

Goal: prepare questions and answer points that stay consistent with submitted materials.

## Protocol

1. Load confirmed profile, target, advisor profile, match report, and generated materials.
2. Generate likely questions from advisor direction, student projects, gaps, and submitted claims.
3. For each question, provide answer points and likely follow-up questions.
4. Mark claims the student should not make because evidence is missing or weak.
5. Keep answers explainable by the student in their own words.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent project details, grades, paper status, or lab fit.
- Do not coach the user to misrepresent experience.
- Prefer concise answer points over polished scripts when facts are uncertain.
